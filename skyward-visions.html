About This Collection

Seeing the World From Above

There is a perspective that forever changes the way you see the earth beneath your feet -- the view from above. Aerial and drone photography strips away the familiar and reveals a world of hidden geometry, sweeping colour fields, and patterns so intricate they seem painted by an unseen hand. This collection is a celebration of that elevated vantage point.

From winding rivers that carve through ancient valleys to the tessellated patchwork of farmland at harvest, each image in this series captures the quiet majesty of our planet as seen from the sky. It is a reminder that beauty often lies not in what is directly before us, but in the grand tapestry that unfolds when we dare to look from a different angle.

Behind the Lens

The Art and Science of Aerial Capture

Aerial photography is a discipline where artistry meets technical precision. Every flight is carefully planned -- studying weather patterns, wind speeds, light angles, and airspace regulations long before the drone ever leaves the ground. The challenge lies in translating the raw grandeur of a bird's-eye view into a composition that resonates with depth, balance, and emotion.

From calibrating camera settings for optimal dynamic range at altitude to navigating the narrow window of perfect light, each image in this collection represents the convergence of meticulous preparation and split-second creative decisions. The result is a body of work that transforms the familiar into the extraordinary, revealing the hidden order and beauty of our world from above.
