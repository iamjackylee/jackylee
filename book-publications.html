Book Publications

Explore the diverse collection of books authored by Jacky Lee, diving deep into the intricacies of photography and the realm of mental health.

The Art of Light

Published 2019

A comprehensive guide to understanding and mastering natural light in photography. This book draws on over two decades of field experience, walking readers through the science and artistry of light -- from the soft glow of golden hour to the dramatic contrasts of storm-lit skies. Packed with practical techniques and stunning visual examples, it is an essential resource for photographers seeking to elevate their craft.

Mindful Lens

Published 2020

Exploring the intersection of photography and mental wellness, offering techniques for using photography as a mindfulness practice. This thoughtful volume examines how the act of seeing, composing, and capturing images can serve as a powerful form of meditation, grounding us in the present moment and nurturing emotional resilience through creative expression.

Hong Kong Through My Eyes

Published 2021

A visual journey through Hong Kong's urban landscapes and hidden natural beauty. From the neon-drenched streets of Mong Kok to the misty peaks of Lantau Island, this book reveals the extraordinary contrasts that define one of the world's most dynamic cities -- seen through the deeply personal and artistic lens of a photographer who calls it home.

Skyward: The Aerial Perspective

Published 2023

A collection of stunning aerial photographs showcasing landscapes from above. Leveraging cutting-edge drone technology and years of flight experience, this book redefines how we perceive the earth beneath us -- revealing hidden patterns, breathtaking symmetry, and the raw grandeur of natural and urban terrains viewed from the sky.
