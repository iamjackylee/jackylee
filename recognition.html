Beyond the Lens

Professional Credentials

A distinguished career spanning photography and the built environment, underpinned by chartered professional standing and a commitment to education.

Chartered Building Engineer

Member of the Chartered Association of Building Engineers (CABE), bringing professional engineering rigour and a deep understanding of the built environment to architectural and structural photography.

Chartered Building Surveyor

Fellow of the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors (RICS), combining surveying expertise with a photographer's eye for detail, space, and the interplay of form and function in the built environment.

Course Tutor - University of Hong Kong

Teaching at HKU, one of Asia's most prestigious universities, delivering courses that bridge professional practice with creative visual arts and inspire the next generation of practitioners.

Judge - International Photo Contests

Invited as a juror for international photography competitions, evaluating and recognising outstanding photographic work across diverse genres and styles on the global stage.
