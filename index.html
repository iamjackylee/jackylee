About the Artist

Jacky Lee

A distinguished visual artist and seasoned photographer hailing from Hong Kong, Jacky Lee brings over two decades of experience to every frame he captures.

A self-taught artist with a distinctive style, Jacky specializes in landscape, nightscape, architectural, black and white, and aerial drone photography. His work has earned international acclaim, including the prestigious National Geographic Photo Contest Winner title in 2017.

Beyond the lens, Jacky is a Chartered Building Engineer and Chartered Building Surveyor (RICS, CABE). He serves as a photography advisor at the Hong Kong Biodiversity Museum and as a course tutor at HKU, nurturing the next generation of visual storytellers.
